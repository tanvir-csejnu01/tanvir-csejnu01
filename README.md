## 🧑‍🎓 About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at **The University of Alabama** (since Fall 2026), where I also serve as a **Graduate Teaching Assistant**. Before starting my doctoral studies, I was an **Assistant Professor** in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at **Jagannath University**, Bangladesh, where I am currently on study leave.

My research focuses on extracting meaningful patterns from real-world data, especially text from social media and online news, using data mining, machine learning, and natural language processing techniques.

- 🎓 Pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at The University of Alabama
